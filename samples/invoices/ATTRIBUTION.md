# Attribution

The 12 invoice images in this folder are a sample of the public dataset **Divya52/indian-gst-invoices** on Hugging Face,
licensed **CC-BY-4.0**. They are synthetic Indian GST invoices with ground truth; the "scan" and "photo" quality tiers are
simulated degradation added by the dataset authors. `ground_truth.json` lists the supplier and buyer GSTIN of each image
(taken from the dataset's labels). Used here only to demonstrate and test the document reader.
